About StockFlow

Inventory software made for electronics retailers and distributors.

Electronics move fast: models change, prices shift and one missing charger can cost a sale. StockFlow gives small shops and wholesalers one secure place to track stock, manage suppliers and see where each product is sold online. Staff accounts can add products and adjust stock, while administrators control deletions and team roles.
